I just dont wanna dump every negative thoughts into these posts. After the last blog, I want to do something to not be drown in my life again - "Your life does not better by chance but by change". 

Yep again, I followed many self improvement videos on youtube, I stay consistent on habit traking for a while ( for 3 4 months), try other discipline "challenges"... Although that I gave up on those things but I can still stick to them in other aspects. Like, getting used to workout, study everyday, more confident in myself. I think that life is not that old game I played. I am having more opportunities and witness many thing to be achieved that the freshman-me can not imagine. Those need me to perform more, focus and invest in more effort, leading to sometimes I miss out my other aspects like family, girlfriend, friends. Last weekend, I drop out the tutoring jobs that I am in charge nearly 10 months, as a consequence ( I believe so, I can not carry on more, or I just weak and could not handle?).

Thats my random complant. Currently I want to take action to back this life game. Last month, I finished the "Learning How to Learn" course (Audit) on Coursera. Honestly, some of the lessons are the same as the video on youtube, and the valuable one is making the plan for a day. Through my observation, if that day already have a plan (have classes at school, meeting in evening...) or when in highschool, I was given the timetable carefully organized with super tight schedule, I just be more productive, feel prepared for those events. Otherwise, I just on autopilot mode, do nothing on normal. So the key are some **pressure** to push me through and have my timetable present at the moment automatically - I can list out my own tasks but if I just lazy I gave up. Therefore, an AI assistant program can provide that.

This is not a tutorial or instruction, just my small diary so I dont go deep into. That system will collect my tasks, data then create a daily timetable for me. The source can be from my Obsidian notes, Google Calendar, Gmail, my LMS, come with the prompt. Then that agent will create a note displaying the result. After using for several days, it feels great. Like having some tasks in front of my face, urge me more to complete and satisfy when ticking the finish boxes. ALthough it needs adjustment to really adapt with my life ( flexible time, unexpected events...), I can just type into the note or tell the AI directly. Oh the model I use is just from the AntiGravity CLI (gemini Pro from Student Account). At first, I try to use the OpenWorker framework by Andrew Nguyen, but I dont want to pay for other model. Maybe I could try to recreate the OpenWoker for my system ? The code is there, why not. 

Lets see how it is in the future. See u in next blog.
