My first impression is the overwhelming AI application running in the business context. Yep, AI is breaking out and deeply embedding inside the operation flow in every company, I belive. We use Lark, developed by ByteDace, a working, all-in-one platform. In my daily basis, I can just tell my agent to do anything through the lark MCP from sending messages, edit the docs, slides in Wiki, set calendar...

Everything is AI-transformed,that's also our team's mission. My overall task is to develop the agentic systems for the company. There are already several active agents serving a particular department while I think the target gonna spread over the whole company.

My current task is to improve the current AI platform to be more consistent and effective. As I'm doing, I learn a lot about setting up a system, production, which different from assignments from school. Moreover, work ethnics sound interesting that I have to update daily standup, have weekly team meeting, live in an office, talk to new people from different ages, background, study fields.
